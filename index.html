Help

Have questions or issues?
How do I use this? ▼
Select a reward. For Roblox rewards, enter your Roblox username. For Nitro rewards, no username is needed. Then complete one quest.
How do I receive my prize? ▼
Roblox rewards are delivered via Roblox giftcard. Nitro rewards are delivered via Discord Nitro gift.
What if I can't complete a quest? ▼
If you're having trouble with a quest, please disable your AdBlocker ⛔ if you have one as they block our quest links. If that doesn't help, try another quest (you only need to complete 1). If none work, visit the site on another device 📱💻 (phone/tablet/PC/laptop) to get different quests, or try again tomorrow.
Why do I have to do a quest? ▼
Our site is funded by our partners - by completing their quests, you help us afford to give free rewards to everyone!
Can I claim more than once? ▼
Yes, you can claim as many times as you want! However, keep in mind that a quest might not work again if you've done it already.
Is this an official website? ▼
No - we are not affiliated with or sponsored by Roblox or Discord. We are a Team of developers, content creators, and enthusiasts working to make Roblox & Discord players happy! 💫
