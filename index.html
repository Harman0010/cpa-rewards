Have questions or issues?

How do I use this? ▾
Select a reward. For Roblox rewards, enter your Roblox username. For Nitro rewards, no username is needed. Then complete one quest.
How do I receive my prize? ▾
After completing a quest, your reward is sent automatically. Robux appears in your account within minutes. Nitro is delivered via email.
What if I can't complete a quest? ▾
Just try another one! There are multiple quests available. If one doesn't work for you, another will. Contact support if you have ongoing issues.
Why do I have to do a quest? ▾
Completing quests is how we fund the rewards. Our partners pay us when you complete tasks, and we share that with you as Robux or Nitro.
Can I claim more than once? ▾
Each account can claim once every 24 hours. Complete more quests to increase your daily limit. Premium accounts get higher limits.
Is this an official Roblox website? ▾
No. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation. We are an independent rewards platform.
